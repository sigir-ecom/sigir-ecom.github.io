SIGIR Workshop on eCommerce will take place on Friday, July 24, 2026 in Melbourne, Australia.

SIGIR eCom'26 Workshop Program

The workshop schedule will be announced closer to the event date.

The program will include:

  • Two invited talks from practitioners and academic experts
  • Moderated panel discussion on the workshop theme
  • Selected long contributed talks with facilitated discussions
  • Poster boaster presentations followed by a poster session
  • Playfully themed lunch tables for casual networking
  • Data challenge session
  • Agents Anonymous group activity for sharing real-world challenges
